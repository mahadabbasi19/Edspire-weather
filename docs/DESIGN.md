# Design research and review

Research performed on 13 November 2026, with actual Chromium navigation and screenshots. `scripts/research.mjs` and `scripts/research-detail.mjs` reproduce the public-page inspection. Third-party captures and raw page extracts stay local and are excluded from publication.

| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

The system remains coherent: forest/sage for identity and primary action, amber for weather, neutral surfaces for information. A single temperature is visually dominant. Type uses DM Sans with a full system fallback. Main grid gaps are 24 px, card padding roughly 20–28 px, controls at least 44 px on mobile. Mobile metrics stack rather than squeezing three cards. Five compact forecast columns remain visible without page overflow.

Product design quality gate: inspect light and dark screens, destination time and source label, prominent search, empty favourites, clear offline/cache status, nearby actionable errors, permission explanation, keyboard search and modal focus. Browser tests assert overflow at 375/600/768/1024/1280. Automated screenshots are reviewed locally; fixture screenshots are identified as test data. Real-network screenshot is separately labelled. A small line icon system avoids inconsistent illustrations. Reduced-motion preferences disable animations.
